# LANTERN

> Carry a lantern through a dark cave, gather the five sparks, and the door at the end opens.

- **Verb:** walk and jump, in the light you carry.
- **Mood:** quiet and warm: a small light in a big dark.
- **Look:** 320 × 180, 16-pixel tiles, a cold blue cave lit only by the lantern, the sparks and, at the end, the open door.
- **Done when:** a player and the bot both reach the open door; the level reads at a glance; the jump feels good.

LANTERN is Polter's example game: everything in it is made with the engine alone (pictures from PNG files and an Aseprite sheet, animation, tiles from a text level, a camera, actions bound in `assets/keys.txt`, the built-in font), and everything in it is MPL-2.0, to read and to copy.
